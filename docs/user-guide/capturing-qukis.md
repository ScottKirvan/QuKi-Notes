# Capturing QuKis

## The editor

The editor is the first thing you see when you open the app. It is always home.

Open QuKi-Notes. Type. That is the full workflow for capture.

## Auto-save

You do not save manually. QuKi-Notes saves automatically:

- **2 seconds** after you stop typing
- **Every 30 seconds** while you are still typing
- **When you leave** the app or switch to another one

There is no save button and no draft state. Your QuKi is always current.

## Creating a new QuKi

From the QuKis list, tap **+** to open a blank editor for a new QuKi.

From the editor, tap **Menu → QuKis** to go to the list, then tap **+**.

## Formatting

A formatting toolbar appears at the bottom of the editor. Full markdown rendering is coming in a future release. Text entered now is preserved as-is.

## Android: share text into QuKi-Notes

On Android, you can share text from any other app directly into QuKi-Notes. Use the standard share sheet and choose **QuKi-Notes**. A new QuKi opens with the shared text pre-loaded.
