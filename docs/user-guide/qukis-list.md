# QuKis List

## Opening the list

Tap the **stacked-pages icon** in the top left of the editor, or tap **Menu → QuKis**.

## What you see

QuKis are listed newest first. Each entry shows:

- A preview of the first line
- How long ago it was last modified (e.g. "just now", "3h ago", "May 14")

## Searching

Type in the **Search** bar at the top to filter by content. Search is case-insensitive. Tap **×** to clear.

## Opening a QuKi

Tap any entry to open it in the editor.

## Deleting a QuKi

Swipe left on a QuKi to delete it. An **Undo** option appears briefly. After that, the QuKi moves to **Recently Deleted**.

## Recently Deleted

Deleted QuKis are held in Recently Deleted until you remove them permanently. Open it from **Settings → Notes → Trash**.

- Tap a QuKi to restore it.
- Swipe to permanently delete it (confirmation required).
