# QuKi Manifesto

## What is a QuKi?

A **QuKi** is a short note, a picture, a thought, a temporary list, a rough draft — captured, quickly and easily, in the moment.

QuKis live in the now. They are not filed, tagged, organized, foldered, or curated. They surface what's current while older QuKis age into irrelevance. They can be a starting point before being sent off to become more important things — temporary lists, things to remember, a whiteboard or pasteboard, a stopping point on their way to becoming emails, notes, skeets, or whatever you need them to be.

QuKis, and this app, embody four key principles:

1. **Velocity** — open the app, type, done. No "+ New", no template picker, no title field, no folder choice. Copious notetakers don't want extra steps and requirements for each capture. The point is often to get something off your mind quickly, without navigating a filesystem or organizational structure to do it.
2. **Open data** — QuKis are yours. Individual, plain-text `.md` files on disk; no proprietary serialization, no cloud lock-in.
3. **Information-first UI** — the QuKi is the interface; minimalism is key. QuKis stress content over layout.
4. **Extensibility** — what you do with a QuKi is personal. It might fade into the list as newer thoughts arrive, or you may want to send it somewhere else — a daily log, a chat, a GitHub issue, a wiki entry — that destination is yours to define.

---

## What is QuKi-Notes?

**QuKi-Notes** is the capture surface. A blank canvas on launch. Just type and go.  Your QuKis are always there when you want them. Transports plugins are there when you're ready to send.

---

## What QuKi-Notes Is NOT

**Quki Notes is NOT:**
- **an organizer.** No projects, no tasks system, no kanban, no calendar.
- **a vault.** No folders. No tags. No backlinks. No graph view. No daily-notes-with-templates.
- **a knowledge base.** No wiki linking, no second-brain rituals and processes.
- **a publishing tool.** Markdown output is a minimalist implementation detail, not a feature.
- **a gatekeeper.** Your QuKis are plain text files on your device. You can read, copy, or move them without opening the app.
